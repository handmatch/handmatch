Terms & refund policy

These are the key terms of your handmatch one-time fee. Our full terms of service will be published on this page before launch. Questions? Email hello.handmatch@gmail.com.

One lifetime fee per hire

You pay once for each person you hire, and never again for that hire. No monthly fees, no markup on every visit. It includes the unlimited options and up to 3 replacements at no additional charge, within 90 days after your first hire starts.

Replacements

If your match isn’t the right fit, just tell our team. We’ll handpick someone new at no extra fee, with up to 3 replacements per hire within 90 days after your first hire starts. One active match at a time.

Refunds

Full refund if you cancel before we share your picks, or if we can’t find a suitable match near you within 14 days. 50% refund if we’ve shared your picks but you haven’t hired anyone within 30 days. Once you’ve hired, the fee isn’t refundable, but if it’s not the right fit you get up to 3 replacements within 90 days at no extra charge. To request a refund, email hello.handmatch@gmail.com.

Hiring directly

You hire and pay your cleaner, housekeeper or nanny directly, at the rate and schedule you agree together. Rates are subject to your state’s minimum wage.
